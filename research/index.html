Research

I do research in computer science with a focus on systems security and privacy.

Broadly, my interests include

  • Systems software security
  • Application security
  • IoT security
  • Formal verification
  • Agentic AI for software engineering
  • Private messaging systems
  • Operating system and browser privacy
  • Applied cryptography
  • Zero-knowledge systems
  • Decentralized and overlay networking
  • Decentralized financial infrastructure

Right now, I’m a CS Ph.D. candidate at Northwestern University advised by Peter Dinda. My work is part of the Privacy Backplane Project and the Buoyancy Project.
